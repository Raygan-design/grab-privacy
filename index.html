Privacy Policy

Grab Media Saver (browser extension) · Effective 17 November 2026

In short: the extension collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of its own. Everything it does happens inside your browser and on your computer.

What the extension looks at

When you use it on a page (by clicking its icon, pressing its shortcut, or clicking its on-page Download button), it reads the addresses of the images and videos on that page, and, on the supported social sites, the media details the page has already loaded. This is used only to show you a preview and to save the file you choose. This information is processed on your device. It is not stored by the extension and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What it stores

  • One setting – whether the on-page Download button is shown – kept in your browser's extension storage. If Chrome Sync is on, Chrome may sync this setting between your own devices; that is handled by Chrome, not by us.
  • Files you download – saved to your computer's Downloads folder (in a folder named “Grab”). The extension does not keep a history of what you download.

Network activity

  • To save a file, the extension requests it from the same website or media server that hosts it, using your browser's normal session, exactly as the page itself would. Nothing is routed through any other server.
  • If you paste a link, the extension opens that link in a minimised browser window so it can read the media on the page, then closes the window.
  • The extension does not contact any server operated by its developer.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or fingerprinting.
  • We do not load or run code from outside the extension package.

Your responsibility

Please only download content you own or have permission to use, and follow the terms of the sites you use. The extension is a convenience tool; it does not grant you any rights to the content you save.
